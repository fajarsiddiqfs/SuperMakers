Our Story

Supermakers started in 2019, when Fajar Siddiq built Shipping Game, an indie game made with no code on Koji. It launched on Product Hunt and ranked #4 of the day, featuring Buy Me A Coffee and Makerlog.

Today, Supermakers has grown from one small game into a studio that builds more products.
